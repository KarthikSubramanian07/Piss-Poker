About Piss Poker

Piss Poker is a free realtime chip tracker for live home poker. The product keeps every stack, bet, side pot, and payout honest while the cards stay on the table in front of you.

Home games lose time to missing chip sets, borrowed stacks, and side-pot arguments. Piss Poker moves the chips onto each player's phone and leaves the deal physical, so shuffling and table talk stay part of the night.

What the tracker does

The site runs in a modern mobile browser. A host starts a table, shares a four-letter code, and players join without creating accounts. The server owns the rules: blinds post themselves, turn order is enforced, minimum raises follow the last full raise, and short all-ins follow TDA-style reopening rules. Side pots layer from what each player put in. Chops let the table mark winners per pot before payout. Undo, proxy seats, and settle-up cover the messy parts of a real living-room game.

Why it is public

The engine, protocol, and UI are open source under the MIT license. The live product is hosted at piss-poker.pages.dev. There is no advertising network inside the table UI and no requirement to buy chips or a subscription to play a home game.
